I contributed to the visual effects for the film [Sinners (2025)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinners_(2025_film)) as part of the Rising Sun Pictures team.

**Key highlights of my work:**
- Developed and deployed machine learning solutions for advanced face replacement, enabling seamless transformation of stunt doubles into Michael B. Jordan’s twin characters.
- Focused on performance transfer, ensuring the digital faces accurately reflected the nuances and expressions of the actors.
- Collaborated with the VFX team to deliver high-quality, photorealistic results for complex action sequences.
- Utilized deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch and advanced Python tools for pipeline development.
